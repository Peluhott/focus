Why Focus

Most to-do lists grow faster than the goals behind them.

Focus starts from the other end. You name the few things you're working toward. Projects, to-dos and notes each point back to one of them, and anything that doesn't gets flagged.

The headline sharpens out of a 10px blur as it enters, the same move as the hero. The paragraph fades up 250ms later.

What makes it different

Most apps help you add more. Focus helps you choose.

1

Goals come first

You set what you're working toward before you write a single task. Lists stay in service of it.

2

It says no for you

A limit on active goals and a flag on to-dos that don't serve one. Taking on everything stops being the default.

3

It helps you begin

When a to-do feels too big, Focus offers one small first step instead of another reminder.
